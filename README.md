## Hi, I'm Maksim 👋

I collect music by the album — 23,000+ tracks, FLAC rips, music videos, live sets — and build software for it and for everyday PC life.
Under the name **Grimsby** I make free offline apps for Windows: a music player, a file converter, a tag editor, a teleprompter, a PC health helper, a video editor for music videos and a personal planner.
I also write about hip-hop and albums in my Telegram channel **[@itsgrimsby](https://t.me/itsgrimsby)** (in Russian).
